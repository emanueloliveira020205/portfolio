About Me

I'm a developer passionate about technology! If there’s a USB-C port, I probably love it! My main focus is frontend and web development. I work with PHP, JavaScript, and Node.js, but I’m always eager to learn new frameworks.

I also have solid knowledge in Linux (despite the occasional headaches it brings...). I gained experience in electronics assembling LED panels, but… honestly, I don’t like getting my hands dirty! 😉

Outside the *shell*, I enjoy exploring social media and dedicating myself to video editing. Creativity never stops!
